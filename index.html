The Grand Horizon Clarity

Shared behavior structure + body-specific modulation = one policy, any body.

All robots perform the same semantic actions — reach, grasp, place. What differs is the physical realization: which joints to activate, how much torque to apply, how far to extend. A single neural policy can learn the shared structure, while a morphology embedding tells it how to instantiate that structure for any specific body.

Horizon Clarity — Theory Roadmap

Each section distills the first-principle truth. The 80% that constitutes the fundamental, sun-setting clarity.

Week 1–2

Diffusion Policy

"Denoising is learning"

Corrupt action sequences with noise, learn to reverse the corruption. Multimodal distributions emerge naturally.

 Week 3

Morphology Conditioning

"Body is a vector"

Encode any robot's physical structure as a descriptor. Condition the policy via FiLM — one network, infinite bodies.

 Week 4

Task Conditioning

"Tasks need names"

Without explicit task signals, multi-task policies collapse to the average of incompatible behaviors.

 Week 5

Data Scaling

"Data is the policy"

Imitation learning can only reproduce what it has seen. Coverage of the state space determines performance.

 Week 6

Ablation Science

"Prove by absence"

Every architectural choice must earn its place. Remove it, measure the loss. The scientific method of deep learning.

 Week 7–8

Transfer & Fine-tuning

"General then specific"

Pre-train wide on many bodies, fine-tune narrow on the target. Prior + update = posterior.

Architecture at a Glance

$$\pi_\theta(a_{1:H} \mid o_{1:T}, m, k) = \text{DDIM}^{-1}\big(\epsilon_\theta(x_t, t, \text{obs}(o), \text{morph}(m), \text{task}(k))\big)$$

A conditional denoising diffusion process generates action chunks, conditioned on visual observations, proprioception, morphology descriptor, and task embedding. The same 52.7M parameter network controls 3 distinct robots across multiple manipulation tasks.
